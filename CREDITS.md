# Credits

## Instrument samples (`public/samples/`)

Used only when "Real instruments" is selected on the title screen (the default sound is the
built-in synthesizer). All files were downloaded and processed by `scripts/fetch-samples.mjs`.

**Changes made to all samples:** leading silence removed, cut to 0.25–3 seconds with a fade-out,
converted to mono MP3 (44.1 kHz, 96 kbit/s). Pitches were verified by measurement; VCSL file names
were shifted up one octave to scientific pitch notation (their "C4" sounds as C5).

### VCSL – Versilian Community Sample Library

- Piano and tenor saxophone in several dynamics (soft / normal / loud, picked by how hard you play)
- Instruments: upright piano (Yamaha), tenor saxophone, harmonica (Hohner Special 20, C),
  snare drum, hi-hat, suspended cymbal (used as ride), claps
- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication) – https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

### FluidR3_GM soundfont

- Instruments: electric guitar (clean), overdriven guitar, electric bass (finger),

- Soundfont: FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen and contributors
- Rendered to MP3 by the project **midi-js-soundfonts** (Benjamin Gleitzman):
